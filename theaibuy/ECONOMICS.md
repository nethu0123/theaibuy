# Economics

This file outlines the assumptions that would need to be true for `theaibuy` to reach $1M ARR within 18 months.

## ARR Target

Target ARR: $1,000,000

Required MRR:

```text
$1,000,000 / 12 = $83,333 MRR
```

## Possible Pricing Model

The current product is a free audit and lead capture tool. To reach $1M ARR, it needs a paid motion after the audit.

Possible paid tiers:

| Tier | Price | Target customer |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter monitoring | $99/month | Small teams that want recurring AI spend checks |
| Growth | $299/month | Startups with 10-50 employees |
| Advisory / managed savings | $1,000-$2,500/month | Larger teams with high savings potential |

## Customer Count Needed

| Average MRR per customer | Customers needed for $83,333 MRR |
| ---: | ---: |
| $99 | 842 |
| $299 | 279 |
| $499 | 167 |
| $833 | 100 |
| $1,500 | 56 |

The most realistic path is not hundreds of tiny accounts. A better path is a blended model where high-savings accounts pay more.

Example blended target:

```text
60 Growth customers at $299 MRR = $17,940 MRR
45 Managed customers at $1,450 MRR = $65,250 MRR
Total = $83,190 MRR
ARR = $998,280
```

That is close to $1M ARR.

## 18-Month Customer Pace

To reach roughly 105 paying customers in 18 months:

```text
105 customers / 18 months = 5.8 net new paying customers per month
```

Because churn will exist, the real target should be 7 to 9 gross new customers per month by the second half of the period.

## Funnel Assumptions

One plausible monthly funnel:

| Step | Rate | Count |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Website visitors | - | 25,000 |
| Start audit | 12% | 3,000 |
| Complete audit | 65% | 1,950 |
| Submit email | 25% | 488 |
| Qualified lead | 20% | 98 |
| Sales/demo/implementation call | 25% | 25 |
| Paid conversion | 25% | 6 |

This funnel would create about 6 customers per month at steady state.

## CAC Assumptions

For the model to work, blended CAC must stay low because the entry product is a free audit.

Target CAC:

- Self-serve customer: $75 to $250
- Growth customer: $300 to $900
- Managed customer: $1,500 to $4,000

Target payback:

- Self-serve: under 3 months
- Growth: under 4 months
- Managed: under 4 months

If the blended customer pays about $793 MRR, a blended CAC under $2,500 gives a payback period around 3.2 months.

```text
$2,500 CAC / $793 MRR = 3.15 months
```

## Conversion Rate Requirements

The product needs one of these to be true:

- High traffic with modest conversion
- Moderate traffic with strong lead capture
- Lower traffic but very high intent and high ACV

The best near-term path is high-intent traffic:

- Pricing comparison searches
- AI subscription waste searches
- Founder/finance audiences
- Direct outbound to startups with visible AI adoption

## What Must Be True

For $1M ARR in 18 months, these assumptions need to hold:

- The audit produces a savings number that feels credible.
- At least 20% to 30% of completed audits submit an email.
- At least 15% to 25% of email leads are qualified.
- High-savings leads are willing to pay for help implementing savings.
- The product can create a paid wedge beyond the free report.
- Average revenue per paying account reaches at least $500 to $800 MRR.
- Churn stays low because the product evolves into recurring monitoring.
- Acquisition includes at least one scalable channel, likely SEO or founder/finance content.
- CAC payback stays under 4 months.

## Main Risk

The main risk is that users treat the audit as a one-time free calculator and do not need recurring software.

The product can reduce that risk by adding:

- Monthly AI spend monitoring
- Price-change alerts
- Team seat audits
- Renewal reminders
- Exportable CFO reports
- Vendor consolidation recommendations
