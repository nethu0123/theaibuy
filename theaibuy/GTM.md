# Go-To-Market

## Exact Target User

The primary target user is an operations-minded founder, finance lead, or engineering manager at a 5 to 100 person startup that already pays for multiple AI tools and does not yet have a formal software procurement process.

They usually have:

- Multiple employees using ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini, or Windsurf
- Duplicate subscriptions across individual cards and company cards
- Little visibility into which tools are actually redundant
- Pressure to reduce burn without slowing the team
- No interest in a heavy enterprise procurement platform

## Best Initial Segment

The best first segment is technical startups with 10 to 50 employees.

Why this segment:

- They adopt AI tools quickly.
- They often have overlapping coding and chatbot subscriptions.
- Their subscription waste is painful but not yet centrally managed.
- A founder, CTO, or ops lead can make a cancellation decision quickly.
- The savings number is easy to understand and share.

## Buyer

Likely buyers:

- Founder or CEO
- COO or ops lead
- Head of Finance
- CTO or VP Engineering

The strongest buyer is the person responsible for reducing software spend, not the person who loves trying new AI tools.

## User

Likely users:

- Founder doing a quick audit
- Finance lead collecting software spend data
- Engineering manager comparing developer AI tools
- Ops generalist cleaning up subscriptions

## Pain

The pain is not "I need another AI tool." The pain is:

- "We are paying for too many AI tools."
- "I do not know which tools overlap."
- "I need a fast, defensible savings number."
- "I need to show leadership a clear action plan."

## Positioning

`theaibuy` is a free AI spend audit that finds duplicate AI subscriptions and oversized plans in under a minute.

## Core Message

Stop guessing which AI tools to cancel. Enter your stack, see your waste, and get a shareable savings report.

## Acquisition Channels

- LinkedIn posts aimed at founders, finance leads, and CTOs
- SEO pages for AI tool pricing comparisons
- Programmatic landing pages for "ChatGPT vs Claude cost", "Cursor vs Copilot cost", and similar searches
- Founder and SaaS communities
- Finance and RevOps newsletters
- Direct outbound to startups hiring heavily for engineering or AI roles

## Lead Magnet

The audit result is the lead magnet.

The product should show value before asking for an email. After the user sees savings, the app asks whether they want the report emailed or shared.

## Conversion Events

- Visitor starts audit
- Visitor completes audit
- Visitor sees savings result
- Visitor submits email
- Visitor shares audit link
- Visitor requests help implementing savings

## Sales Motion

Start self-serve and lead-gen first.

Possible monetization paths:

- Paid implementation help for high-savings teams
- Subscription monitoring for ongoing AI spend
- Affiliate or partner revenue from recommended tools
- B2B dashboard for finance teams
- Procurement advisory package for larger accounts

## Wedge

The wedge is a fast, free audit with a useful savings number. The expansion path is ongoing monitoring and cancellation/recommendation workflow.
