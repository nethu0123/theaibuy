# Metrics

`theaibuy` is best measured as a B2B lead-generation and qualification tool first, and a SaaS product second.

## North Star Metric

Qualified savings leads per month.

A qualified savings lead is a completed audit with:

- Valid business email
- Company or role signal
- Meaningful estimated savings
- Clear tool stack data
- Permission to follow up

## Core Funnel Metrics

| Metric | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Visitors | Total people landing on the site |
| Audit start rate | Percent of visitors who begin entering their stack |
| Audit completion rate | Percent of starters who submit an audit |
| Result view rate | Percent of completed audits that successfully see results |
| Email capture rate | Percent of result viewers who submit email |
| Share rate | Percent of result viewers who copy or share a public audit |
| Qualified lead rate | Percent of captured leads that match target customer criteria |
| Sales accepted lead rate | Percent of qualified leads worth direct follow-up |
| Customer conversion rate | Percent of qualified leads that become paying customers |

## Product Quality Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Median estimated monthly savings | Shows whether the tool is finding meaningful pain |
| Percent of audits with savings above $500/month | Identifies high-intent accounts |
| Recommendation acceptance rate | Shows whether users trust the output |
| Public report copy rate | Shows whether the result is worth sharing |
| Email report request rate | Shows whether value is strong enough for lead capture |
| Repeat audit rate | Shows whether users come back after changing their stack |

## B2B Lead Quality Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Business email percentage | Separates business users from casual consumers |
| Company domain quality | Helps identify real accounts |
| Team size distribution | Confirms fit with the target segment |
| Role distribution | Shows whether buyers or only end users are arriving |
| Tool count per audit | More tools usually means stronger savings opportunity |
| High-savings account percentage | Indicates potential for managed or advisory revenue |

## Acquisition Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Channel-level visitor count | Shows where traffic comes from |
| Channel-level audit start rate | Shows which channels attract intent |
| Channel-level email capture rate | Shows which channels create leads |
| CAC by channel | Shows whether acquisition can scale profitably |
| SEO landing page conversion | Measures pricing-comparison and alternative pages |
| Content assisted conversions | Shows whether founder/finance content is working |

## Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Lead-to-customer conversion rate | Core B2B funnel efficiency |
| Average revenue per account | Determines whether $1M ARR is realistic |
| CAC payback period | Measures capital efficiency |
| Gross margin | Should be high if AI calls are controlled |
| Monthly churn | Shows whether the product has recurring value |
| Net revenue retention | Shows whether accounts expand over time |
| Sales cycle length | Important for hitting an 18-month ARR goal |

## Suggested Event Tracking

Track these product events:

```text
page_view
audit_started

tool_row_removed
audit_submitted
audit_completed
audit_failed
result_viewed
email_submitted
email_sent
share_link_copied
public_audit_viewed
high_savings_detected
```

## Target Benchmarks

Early target benchmarks:

| Metric | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Audit start rate | 10% to 15% |
| Audit completion rate | 60% to 75% |
| Email capture rate after result | 20% to 30% |
| Qualified lead rate | 15% to 25% |
| Qualified lead to customer | 5% to 15% |
| CAC payback | Under 4 months |
| Monthly churn | Under 3% |

## Dashboard Priority

The first dashboard should show:

- Visitors
- Completed audits
- Captured emails
- Qualified leads
- High-savings leads
- Average monthly savings found
- Lead-to-customer conversion
- CAC by channel
